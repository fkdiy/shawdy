## What constitutes abusive use?

[**Illegal content:**]{.text-highlighted} Creating short URLs that link to
websites containing illegal content.

[**Discrimination and hate speech:**]{.text-highlighted} Linking to websites
that demean individuals or groups based on their origin, religion,
sexuality, identity, gender, or disability.

[**Incitement to hatred:**]{.text-highlighted} Linking to websites that incite
hatred against parts of the population or violate the human dignity of
others.

[**Fraud and deception:**]{.text-highlighted} Using Shawdy to conceal
fraudulent intent or impersonate other services, companies, or public
authorities.
